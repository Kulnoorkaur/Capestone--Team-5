# AI Regulatory Readiness Platform for Imaging-Informed AI

## Project Overview

This project aims to develop a software platform that helps developers of AI-enabled medical imaging technologies better understand and prepare for regulatory requirements in Canada, the United States, and the European Union.

Regulatory requirements for AI-enabled medical devices can be difficult to navigate, especially since they vary depending on the device's intended purpose, clinical role, risk classification, and target jurisdiction.

The proposed platform will help users identify potentially applicable regulatory pathways, assess their current level of readiness, and determine what documentation or additional work may be needed as they move through the development process.

The platform is intended to support early-stage regulatory planning rather than replace formal regulatory assessments or decisions made by regulatory authorities.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of the project are to:

1. **Develop an AI Model Profile** to collect relevant information about the user's AI system, including its intended purpose, clinical application, and functionality.
2. **Provide preliminary regulatory screening** to help identify potential medical device status, classification, and regulatory pathways across different jurisdictions.
3. **Develop a stage-gated readiness assessment** to evaluate whether key regulatory requirements have been addressed.
4. **Track regulatory documentation** to identify available evidence, missing information, and areas requiring further work.
5. **Generate a personalized regulatory roadmap** that summarizes assessment results and outlines recommended next steps.

## Proposed Platform Structure

The platform is planned around five main components:

### 1. AI Model Profile

Collects information about the AI system, including its intended use, target users, patient population, clinical setting, and AI functionality. This information will serve as the starting point for the regulatory assessments.

### 2. Preliminary Regulatory Screening

Uses information from the AI Model Profile to help identify potentially applicable regulatory requirements, including preliminary device classification and regulatory pathways.

### 3. Stage-Gated Readiness Assessment

Evaluates the AI system's regulatory readiness across key development areas, such as dataset quality, risk management, technical validation, clinical evidence, and post-market monitoring.

### 4. Documentation Tracking

Allows users to track supporting documentation and identify missing evidence or information needed to address regulatory requirements.

### 5. Personalized Regulatory Roadmap

Provides a summary of the assessment results, identifies areas that require further work, and outlines possible next steps toward regulatory readiness.

## Regulatory Frameworks

The platform will focus on three main jurisdictions:

* **Canada:** Health Canada medical device regulations and relevant guidance for Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) and Machine Learning-enabled Medical Devices (MLMD).
* **United States:** FDA regulations and guidance for AI-enabled medical device software.
* **European Union:** EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR) and applicable requirements under the EU Artificial Intelligence Act.

The regulatory research will be used to develop structured requirements, assessment criteria, and decision logic for the platform.
